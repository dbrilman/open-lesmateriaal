# Open lesmateriaal – Mevrouw Brilman

Free, open educational resources for Dutch secondary (vo) and vocational (mbo) education, by Denise Brilman, educational technologist. Everything here was built with Claude, without a programming background, and is meant to be used, adapted and shared by teachers.

*Nederlands: gratis, open lesmateriaal voor vo en mbo over AI-geletterdheid, nepnieuws, geldgeletterdheid en duurzaamheid.*

## Projects

| Project | What it is | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Is dit waar?** | A free non-profit chatbot where students check fake news (text and images, with sources), plus lesson materials | [isditwaar.nl](https://isditwaar.nl) |
| **AI gesnapt** | 40 AI-literacy posters for the staff room and the "AI Ontcijferd" kraakkit, an AI-literacy booklet for students | [aigesnapt.nl](https://aigesnapt.nl) |
| **Geld gesnapt** | Open, free money-literacy material for students | [geldgesnapt.nl](https://geldgesnapt.nl) |
| **Duurzaamheid in de klas** | Free sustainability lessons and digibord games; the game Impact Imperium is played in 25+ schools in 5 countries | [duurzaamheidindeklas.nl](https://duurzaamheidindeklas.nl) |

## Why

Teachers rarely have time to build good material on AI, fake news and money. These resources are free, in Dutch, and ready to use in class without preparation.
